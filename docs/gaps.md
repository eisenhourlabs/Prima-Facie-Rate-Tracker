# Manual checks and data gaps

What the tracker cannot do automatically, and history it is missing. Each item says why and
what would close it. Update this file when an item is closed or a new one is found. Reasons
for each decision are in `docs/decisions.md`.

Last updated: 2026-11-21.

## States checked by hand (not monitored automatically)

These states appear on the page under "Not monitored automatically". A new notice there is
not detected by the scheduled check; it must be looked for by hand.

| State | Why | Where to look | What would close it |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arizona | difi.az.gov and publicmeetings.az.gov answer HTTP 403 to the tool (Cloudflare bot check). Getting past it is ruled out | Department of Insurance and Financial Institutions orders setting prima facie rates (A.A.C. R20-6-604.03 to .05; A.R.S. 20-1621.05), at least every three years; hearing notices on publicmeetings.az.gov | The site stops refusing the tool, or another official host of the orders is found. Seed history needs the orders saved by hand |
| Colorado | doi.colorado.gov answers the tool with HTTP 403 ("Request blocked", CloudFront), including its document files: a technical refusal. The official rule text is on the Secretary of State's Code of Colorado Regulations (coloradosos.gov/CCR), whose robots.txt allows the tool but whose terms of use say the site "is intended for use by natural persons" and prohibit "computerized 'robots' or 'data mining'", and reproduction without written permission. Not used for monitoring or saved copies | Regulation 4-9-2 (3 CCR 702-4 Series 4-9), Appendix A "Summary of Component Based Rates": a new rule version on the CCR page for Series 4-9, proposed rules in the Colorado Register and eDocket, and the Division's regulations page | Written permission from the Secretary of State, or another official host that admits the tool, or an owner decision |
| Delaware | insurance.delaware.gov and delaware.gov answer the tool (and a plain web reader) with a firewall "Request Rejected" page: a refusal. The regulations system launched May 19, 2025 (regulations.delaware.gov, which also hosts the Register of Regulations) answers /robots.txt with its app page, which the tool treats as unreadable (the same question as Alabama's admin code). The archive (archive.regulations.delaware.gov) is readable but frozen since May 2025 | Regulation 1701 section 2.0; proposed amendments in the Delaware Register of Regulations | Owner decided 2026-11-20 that the app page counts as no robots.txt; still to do: read regulations.delaware.gov's terms, then add the Register and the 1701 page (a single-page app with /api/ addresses) as sources |

Not used for monitoring or saved copies, as for Colorado | HAR chapter 16-6 (credit life 16-6-11, credit disability 16-6-12 and its exhibit dated July 1, 1988); the Division's copy is files.hawaii.gov/dcca/ins/har/ins_har_6-c.pdf. | Minnesota | Only the rule chapters 2760 and 2761 are watched (lagging). | Virginia | www.scc.virginia.gov robots.txt admits named search engines only, deliberately; its web policy limits use to "intended purposes". Not overridden | State Corporation Commission orders for each triennium (Va. Code 38.2-3725, 38.2-3730) | An owner decision to override, or another official host. No seed records yet |

unmonitored (see the page and `config/sources.csv`). New Mexico and South Carolina have seed
